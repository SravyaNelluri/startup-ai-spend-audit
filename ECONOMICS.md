\# Economics



This project can work as a lead generation tool for Credex. The app is useful because the user gives information about current AI spend, team size, and possible savings. This makes the lead more qualified than a normal website visitor.



\## Simple funnel assumption



Assume:



\- 100 people visit the website

\- 40 people complete the audit

\- 15 people enter their email

\- 5 people are qualified leads

\- 1 person becomes a customer



If one customer is worth around $2,000 per year in gross profit, even a small conversion rate can make the product useful.



\## Customer acquisition cost



At the start, CAC can be low because the first users can come from direct outreach, LinkedIn posts, founder communities, and Credex’s existing network.



The real cost is time. For example, if 3 hours of outreach leads to 2 qualified calls, the cash cost is almost zero, but the founder time still matters.



\## Path to $1M ARR



To reach $1M ARR, Credex would need either:



\- 500 customers worth $2,000/year each, or

\- 100 customers worth $10,000/year each



The product should focus on users with meaningful savings because they are more likely to convert.



\## Most important metric



The most important metric is qualified savings leads per week. Traffic alone is not enough. A small number of serious startup teams with high AI spend is more valuable than many casual visitors.
