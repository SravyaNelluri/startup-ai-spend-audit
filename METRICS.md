\# Metrics



\## North Star Metric



Qualified savings leads per week.



I chose this because the product is not something users will open every day. It is mainly a lead-generation and savings discovery tool.



\## Input metrics



1\. Homepage visits

2\. Audit starts

3\. Audit completions

4\. Result page views

5\. Email captures

6\. High-savings audits

7\. Copy result URL clicks



\## What I would track first



I would first track:



\- How many people start the audit

\- How many complete the audit

\- How many enter email after seeing the result

\- How many audits show more than $500/month savings



\## Pivot trigger



If many people visit but very few complete the audit, the form is probably too confusing.



If many people complete the audit but nobody enters email, the result page may not feel valuable enough.



If most audits show almost no savings, the target audience or calculation logic needs to change.
